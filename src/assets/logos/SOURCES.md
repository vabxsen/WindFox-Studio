# App logo sources

These logos identify the apps WindFox Studio can install. They are trademarks of
their respective owners, are **not** covered by WindFox Studio's GPL-3.0 license,
and their use here does not imply endorsement by those companies.

Each logo was taken from the app's official website or official source repository
where available, otherwise from Wikimedia Commons. Every logo was resized to
128 x 128 px with transparent padding; square tile icons got rounded corners, and
dark logos on transparent backgrounds were placed on a white tile so they stay
visible in dark mode. Nothing else was changed.
